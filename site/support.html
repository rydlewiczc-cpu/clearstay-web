Clearstay Help & FAQ

Everything you need for offline border tracking, Schengen & tax compliance, encrypted Vault security, and optional Live Briefings.

Why does Clearstay request "Always" Location access?

Allowing Always lets iOS wake Clearstay briefly in the background when you cross a border or enter coastal/offshore waters so your daily logbook stays accurate automatically — even in Airplane Mode or mid-ocean. All GPS fixes are resolved offline on your iPhone and never uploaded.

How do I back up or transfer Clearstay to a new iPhone?

Go to Settings → Backup & Reports, print or save your 24-character Recovery Key safely offline, and export an encrypted .dicbackup archive. Open that archive in Clearstay on your new iPhone and enter your Recovery Key to restore your logbook, passport, and encrypted Vault documents.

How do I turn Live Briefings & FX on or off?

Tap Live Briefings & FX on the Home screen or in Settings. It starts OFF (0 bytes sent). Once enabled, cached FX rates work completely offline in Airplane Mode. You can tap the slider icon in Live Briefings anytime to turn it off and delete the local cache.

Important Disclaimer

Clearstay helps you keep accurate presence records and projections for Schengen 90/180, UK SRT, US SPT, and custom thresholds, but is not legal or tax advice. Always verify official tax or immigration filings with a qualified advisor.
